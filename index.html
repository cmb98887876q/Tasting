About ITI Master

ITI Master is a modern, high-performance online mock test platform specifically designed for ITI (Industrial Training Institute) students preparing for NCVT All India Trade Test (AITT) Computer Based Examinations (CBT).

Platform Highlights

  • Comprehensive question banks across core trades like Electrician, Fitter, Welder, and COPA.
  • Standard CBT exam simulation with real-time countdown timer and question palette states.
  • Dual-language support (English and Hindi) matching NCVT guidelines.
  • Instant score calculation, percentage analysis, and detailed answer review system.
